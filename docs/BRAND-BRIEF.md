# Brand brief — working document

## Status

**Threshold is a working name, not the final brand.**

The current name describes an important concept in separation-anxiety training, but that
also makes it generic. Before a public launch the final name should pass domain, search,
social-handle and trade-mark checks.

Do not commission or lock a final logo until the name is chosen.

## Audience

Primary: ordinary dog owners dealing with separation-related distress who need a calm,
practical way to structure and record gradual training.

Secondary:
- trainers and behaviour professionals who may receive shared progress later;
- partners/household members supporting the same dog.

## Desired brand qualities

- calm
- reassuring
- clear
- modern
- evidence-aware
- humane
- trustworthy
- not clinical or cold
- not childish or novelty "dog app" branding

## Avoid

- paw-print logo clichés as the entire identity;
- alarm/red warning palettes;
- medical claims;
- language implying guaranteed treatment or a cure;
- guilt, streak pressure or competitive gamification;
- generic AI wellness gradients;
- names that are difficult to spell after hearing once.

## Naming criteria

A strong candidate should:

1. be easy to say, spell and remember;
2. make sense in the UK but not block international use;
3. feel relevant to calm alone-time/separation progress without needing a paragraph of
   explanation;
4. be distinctive enough to search;
5. have a practical domain path;
6. avoid confusing overlap with established dog-training or behaviour apps;
7. support a simple app icon and wordmark;
8. still make sense if trainer-sharing or household features are added later.

## Positioning direction

Not:
> an AI dog therapist

Not:
> a timer for leaving your dog

Closer to:
> a calm, structured companion for building comfortable alone time and keeping track of
> what genuinely works for your dog.

## Visual direction to explore after naming

- warm neutral base rather than stark white;
- one confident, calming accent colour;
- approachable but restrained typography;
- visual language based on space, progress and calm transitions rather than cartoon dogs;
- dog photography used sparingly and genuinely;
- app icon that remains legible at small PWA/mobile sizes.

## Domain strategy

During development use a South West Websites-controlled preview/production subdomain.
Buy the final product domain only once the naming shortlist has passed availability and
trade-mark checks.

The app architecture must not hard-code the word "Threshold" into user data or database
identifiers where a future rename would create migration work.
